The brand had built a mature western fast-food industry chain in South China with stable offline store operations. The project upgraded the membership system, marketing playbooks and user experience to achieve the strategic goal of digitalized, refined member operations, building a vigorous, youthful brand image for younger consumers to unlock incremental growth.

## Outcomes

- After full-store rollout, over 65% of consumers chose to order online.
- Accumulated 4.7M+ member assets; heavy consumers with 7+ monthly purchases contributed 20% of the brand's orders, with monthly purchase frequency 13.6% above the industry average.
- Member order value in 2023 grew 51% period-over-period, with a 71% activation rate among purchasing members.
- After the paid-member benefits launch, helped the brand achieve bidirectional traffic between online paid conversion and offline in-store redemption, lifting new purchasing users 10% period-over-period and member spend by 7%.
- B2B business revenue in South China reached 1.2M+ in 2023, with 1,900+ average monthly active enterprise customers.

## Responsibilities

- Marketing discount model upgrade with vertical playbooks: upgraded the discount model and marketing engine for fast-food-specific add-on/replacement scenarios, supporting shared or mutually exclusive discounts on add-on (replacement) items, seamlessly moving the offline experience online while preserving business characteristics.
- Multiple transaction system integration: designed the transaction system integration solution and completed open-platform API design for the marketing domain, supporting integration with the brand's POS, ordering mini-program, third-party voucher platforms and bank co-marketing voucher platforms; achieved multi-channel member information accumulation and full-chain query, acquisition and redemption of member assets (coupons and stored value).
- New paid-member benefit types for a benefit matrix: on top of the original one-time gift pack, added transaction-exclusive pricing, periodic gift packs and co-branded benefits, helping the brand create paid benefits such as the Le Ju Card and Tong Chi Gift Pack.
- ToB stored-value gift card solution: built on consumer-side gift card basics, delivered enterprise-side employee management, stored-value management and brand-side settlement modules, closing the loop of enterprise self-service top-up and periodic brand settlement; helped the brand open a new growth business in the B2B market.

**Stack**: CRM, Marketing Engine, Vouchers & Coupons, Stored Value, Open Platform API, POS, Mini-program
