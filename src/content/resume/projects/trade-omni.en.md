The original marketing middle platform was a member private-domain operation product deeply tied to the WeChat ecosystem. The company wanted to enhance transaction fulfillment and customer acquisition/retention by connecting multiple platform channels, exploring public-to-private member operation solutions to raise product competitiveness.

## Outcomes

- Trade Cloud omni-channel transaction platform fully launched: upgraded store, product, price, order and delivery center capabilities; added self-operated channel integrations for Alipay, UnionPay QuickPass and Douyin, providing SaaS merchants with omni-channel transaction fulfillment management.
- Partnered with the Douyin live-streaming ecosystem to help the tea brand unlock public-domain traffic value: delivered a full-chain promotion-purchase-redemption voucher solution in Douyin live rooms - brand live-room GMV reached 16.08M+, 560K+ new members, member repurchase rate up 30%, average order value well above the industry level and double versus the WeChat mini-program.

## Responsibilities

- Omni-channel upgrade of platform order fulfillment: multi-channel transformation from store and product master data to order-taking and delivery fulfillment, enabling the brand to open different channels and service types.
- Open-platform API design and third-party platform integration: led DTO definitions for store, product, transaction and organization centers and open-platform API design; integrated with Meituan, Ele.me, Feishu, WeCom and DingTalk to open data flows in transaction management.
- Product center upgrade with a differentiated management model: through modules for basic product information, product master data, shelf/menu information, price schemes and in-store selling information, achieved unified multi-channel, multi-business-type product management with differentiated information management by transaction scenario.
- Filled capability gaps for new local-life business formats: added shelf management, group-buying order management and live-room management modules to keep pace with the rapid rise of retail and live-shopping formats, giving brand merchants handy operation management tools.

**Stack**: Open Platform API, Alipay, UnionPay QuickPass, Douyin Live, Meituan, Ele.me, Feishu, WeCom, DingTalk
