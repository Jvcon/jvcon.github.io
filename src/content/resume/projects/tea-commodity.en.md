As stores expanded rapidly, management cost and difficulty kept rising. The enterprise adopted a management strategy moving from extensive to refined management and from scattered construction to centralized planning, driving the demand for a digital system upgrade to support business growth.

## Outcomes

- The product center quickly responds to differentiated management demands across multiple transaction channels and scenarios; BOM and cost/material models were extended to further strengthen the product center's management capabilities.
- Helped the brand shift from managing 10+ separate product-related systems to unified product-material-cost full-link management, normalizing product business data and effectively reducing enterprise management costs.
- Enabled the brand to effectively track raw material consumption and supply/purchase data, supporting the optimization of its production plans.

## Responsibilities

- Solution design for building a product center with one-product-multiple-openings: upgraded the brand's product data model to uniformly manage master product information while supporting differentiated sales information management by channel and shelf.
- Designed a highly available product model for rapid omni-channel integration, shortening channel onboarding cycles, migrating existing channel product data, and quickly connecting local-life channels such as Meituan online ordering, Kuaishou, Douyin and Alipay group-buying, with one-click control of omni-channel product information, stock and on/off-shelf status.
- Built the BOM and material cost model: unified management of the bill of materials and cost information for every freshly-made tea product.
- Connected upstream and downstream systems via standard open-platform APIs, integrating POS, store inventory counting and supply management systems, and captured raw material consumption, production cost and store inventory data for sold products, providing more accurate data support for brand R&D and production planning decisions.

**Stack**: Open Platform API, BOM & Material Cost, Product Model Design, System Integration, Omni-channel
