As a fast-rising new-tea brand heading for IPO with over 7,000 stores, the enterprise had made meticulous top-level digital design since founding and accumulated rich data over the years, yet still faced common problems of fast-expanding chains such as non-unified data standards, low data timeliness, poor data sharing and low collaboration efficiency. The project delivered a complete data-middle-platform plus data-application integrated data intelligence solution for the brand.

## Outcomes

- Unified business data integration standards: defined data integration specifications supporting both incremental and full data collection modes, helping the brand quickly integrate data from 9+ business systems.
- Supported data platform construction: built a standard data warehouse and a unified data development platform, handling 170T+ of historical data.
- Established the data system: designed a data statistics metric system covering 13 domains aligned with the brand's business characteristics.
- Completed data applications: based on the data middle platform, designed 24 business-themed analysis scenarios and launched three applications - the data analytics portal, business performance cockpit and operations cockpit.

## Responsibilities

- Business metric consolidation: systematically sorted the metrics and tags involved in business decisions across five data domains (store, product, transaction, member, marketing), defining six attribute dimensions (basic, business, statistics, management, implementation and task) for each metric, providing views for different roles: producers, users, maintainers and managers.
- Data system construction: following the approach of grasp the whole, examine the structure, review the process and study the details, metrics were organized into four levels - a unique first level tightly aligned with business goals, a second level mapping components of first-level goals to department performance, a third level reflecting business process links, and a fourth level covering statistical details - building a unified metric management system for operations departments.
- Data analytics portal design: designed 12 themed analytical reports per actual business needs; for the departments' multi-theme, multi-level, multi-dimension viewing patterns, designed a solution supporting multi-level report drill-down, cross-domain synchronized filtering and dynamic report metrics, greatly reducing business users' learning cost and the difficulty of reading data.

**Stack**: Data Middle Platform, Data Warehouse, Data Integration, BI / Dashboards, KPI System
