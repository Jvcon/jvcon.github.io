The Candao business middle platform abstracts business needs and consolidates functionality for the restaurant industry, providing common services to internal business lines through modules including the aggregation layer, integration layer, common business layer and domain business layers. It connects Candao's and third-party suppliers' business systems, serves over 300 brands, breaks information silos between systems and provides a solid foundation for omni-channel data analytics.

## Responsibilities

- Business middle platform product planning: as the lead of the common business layer, responsible for commonality analysis, framework planning and R&D iteration management for the common and domain business layers.
- Mapping management and conversion mechanism design in the common business layer: dynamically adapted to standard and customized integration needs of suppliers and third-party platforms, forming a standard OneID system.
- Core domain center planning and design: responsible for planning and solution design of merchant, account, dish and transaction centers across domain business layers, streamlining the business architecture, extracting common business abstractions, reducing duplicated feature construction and effectively lowering marginal product/R&D cost.
- Unified account system: built a unified account system based on RBAC-ABAC, centrally managing functional and data permissions across business systems.

**Stack**: Business Middle Platform, OneID, RBAC, ABAC, Service Architecture
