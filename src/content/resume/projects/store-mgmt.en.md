The restaurant store management system consists of three applications: Weican Assistant HD - the core store app integrating table ordering, fast-food ordering, online ordering, takeout order-taking, member management, dish management and shift settlement; WeiPrint - the print-service dispatch center carrying print tasks from multiple app terminals, uniformly managing and scheduling external print hardware for cross-device, multi-template, multi-scenario printing over LAN; and the Merchant Console - a backend integrating feature activation, store management, dish information, member marketing and inventory management.

## Responsibilities

- Fully owned the Weican Assistant HD project from internal beta to official release and subsequent iterations, producing about 48 prototype and PRD documents and completing 63 requirement iterations.
- Built the member marketing module from 0 to 1 with an RFM-based membership marketing system supporting multiple dining marketing campaigns and integrated with mainstream takeout platforms' marketing playbooks.
- Led the plug-in design of functional modules to support combined module pricing, backing the operations team's product marketing strategy.
- Proposed a LAN service-discovery device pairing solution, cutting user cost and improving installation team deployment efficiency - network hardware setup reduced from 1 hour to within 20 minutes.
- Re-planned the client terminal - WeiPrint - printer printing model; after three iterations the order-issue rate rose 6% to about 98.74%.
- Led the PWA transformation of the merchant console, retaining easy access and indexability while gaining near-native interaction experience and offline caching, lowering the usage barrier.

**Stack**: Android App, LAN Device Discovery, Print Service, PWA, RFM, Plugin Architecture
