Served a strategic-cooperation tea-chain brand client, building an omni-channel restaurant management system that connects takeout, mini-program and dine-in order channels, rapidly integrates the membership system, and achieves unified management of stores, dishes, orders, members and campaigns.

## Responsibilities

- As the product line owner, led the product from 0 to 1 and its subsequent planning, requirement design and management, delivering it online for client acceptance.
- Primarily responsible for core feature design of the dish maintenance, order management and member marketing modules.
- Optimized the dish/menu management module based on research into tea-drink business needs, solving menu seasonality pain points - operators maintain only ~700 object records to intensively manage menus across 4,000+ stores, cutting the operation cost of seasonal menu adjustments.
- Designed and implemented an object-oriented unified review-and-publish solution that selectively syncs information to target channels as needed, ensuring the accuracy of object information synchronization.
- Led the refactor of the account permission system with a data-permission rule module that adapts to merchants' business needs, adjusting data permissions per account/account group to meet chain groups' data isolation and security requirements.

**Stack**: Mini-program, Takeout / O2O, Membership, Menu Management, RBAC / Data Permissions
