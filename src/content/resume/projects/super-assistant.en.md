The Super Assistant (ToB) mobile app aims to be an all-in-one store operation tool for chain stores. The philosophy of operating stores like C-end consumers runs through the entire product planning and feature design.

## Outcomes

- Defined and proposed a store operation inspection model, building the Smart Store Inspection module that keeps brands, regional managers and store managers informed of store operations, bringing data-driven decision support capabilities and tools directly to stores.
- Adapted the entire business reporting module for mobile devices so store operators can view data anytime, anywhere, grasp store performance in time, and make timely strategy and management adjustments.
- Streamlined and mobilized frequently used operation functions such as transaction management and marketing operations for store scenarios.

## Responsibilities

- Industrialized and productized the metric system: based on past project practice, distilled metrics across store, product, user, transaction and marketing into an industry metric system and launched a standardized mobile reporting module aligned with industry development.
- Built the store operation inspection model: combined the brand's goal management with store operation KPI data, scoring store operation status across dimensions including user, marketing, operation, product, control and supply capability, and linked these to underlying business actions and management tools to provide corrective measures and suggestions to store operators.
- Multi-domain reports with mobile interaction adaptation: established a drill-down interaction standard for data reports on mobile, reducing the difficulty of viewing data on mobile devices.

**Stack**: Mobile App, Mobile BI / Reports, KPI & Metric System, Drill-down Interaction
