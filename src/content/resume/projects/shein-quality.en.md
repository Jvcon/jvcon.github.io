Targeting two goals — improving the return rate and lowering inspection cost — this project moved apparel bulk production quality from sample-based judgment to risk tiering plus strategy-driven control: quality standards, risk scoring, sampling audit and supplier self-inspection were wired into one chain, so inspection capacity follows risk instead of being spread evenly across orders.

## Responsibilities

- Quality management foundation: mapped quality BOP against PDCA and defined an application framework of strategy templates, base rules, risk/defect/incident libraries, process orchestration and metric monitoring; shipped the base rule and tag libraries to unify scenario scoping across free-inspection, inspection and audit.
- Free-inspection eligibility: identified orders eligible for inspection exemption by SKC risk and historical inspection performance, raising the free-inspection rate by 8.12% (101.5% of target) and removing 3.5 million inspection orders.
- Smart audit: anchored on stratified sampling, injected business constraints by supplier-park-sub-warehouse buckets, and decoupled policy from execution through a risk feature framework, meeting both return-order and supplier coverage targets within audit capacity.
- SKC risk model propagation: pushed risk scores to frontline operations and merchants so inspection and control capacity shifted toward high-risk SKCs.
- Supplier self-inspection: built the merchant self-inspection tool and operating flow to move risk control upstream to suppliers.

## Results

RMB 29.37 million in inspection labor saved; quality return rate improved 5.0% under the same policy (100% of target); RMB 140 million of GMV protected. The project received the Outstanding Team Contribution Award.

**Core Competencies**: Quality Domain Framework Design (PDCA → Strategy → Rules → Metrics), Rule & Policy Abstraction, Risk Tiering & Sampling Modeling, Multi-objective Trade-offs (Cost / Returns / Coverage), Interpretable Delivery of Business Constraints
