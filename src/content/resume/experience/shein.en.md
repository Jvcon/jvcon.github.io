## QMC Quality Management · Apr 2025–Mar 2026

- End-to-end bulk production quality management (lead product owner): led four modules — free-inspection eligibility, smart audit, SKC risk model propagation and supplier self-inspection — raising the free-inspection rate by 8.12%, removing 3.5 million inspection orders, saving RMB 29.37 million in inspection labor, improving the quality return rate by 5.0% and protecting RMB 140 million of GMV.
- Quality management foundation: mapped quality BOP against PDCA and defined an application framework of strategy templates, base rules, risk/defect/incident libraries, process orchestration and metric monitoring; shipped the rule and tag libraries to unify scenario scoping.
- Quality standard library and SOPs: consolidated quality requirements scattered across categories and business lines into a structured standard library, and turned inspection operations into configurable SOPs.
- Return traceability and quality appeals: traced return reasons by product and batch back to inspection records and suppliers, and designed the intake and handling flow for quality appeals so outcomes flow back into standards and supplier records.
